3. **Section 1.6 Overhaul (Post-Show Field Egress & Zero-Doubling-Back Ballast Sweep):**
   - Replaced single-cart sequential egress with the **Five-Step Rapid Field Egress Protocol**:
     1. **Final Chord Collapse:** At show conclusion, 16 student performers immediately disengage arm clips (I) and fold screens flat.
     2. **Ballast Placement:** Students remove ballast bags and place them carefully on the turf without dropping or throwing.
     3. **Hand-Carry Sprint:** Pairs of students hand-carry collapsed screens (13 lbs/student) directly off the field to the stadium exit gate without waiting for carts (~35–45s).
     4. **Directional Ballast Sweep:** Cart pushers enter from the far end (away from exit gate) and sweep along the front sideline toward the exit gate, loading ballast into carts without backtracking.
     5. **Clock Stop & Off-Field Reload:** Carts cross the stadium exit boundary to stop the official CBA 2:00 egress clock; full secure reload of screens onto carts occurs off-clock in the parking/warmup zone.
