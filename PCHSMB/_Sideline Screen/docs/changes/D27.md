## Change

1. **Appendix B: Vinyl Installation Section:**
   - Replaced placeholder notice with comprehensive, shop-ready vinyl installation procedure:
     - Section status callout marking initial working draft for vinyl installation.
     - Subsection **Recommended Vinyl Dimensions & Ordering Specifications** containing an engineering specification table for nominal frame size (48" x 96"), visible image size (TBD), bleed wrap margin (TBD), overall flat cut size (TBD), substrate material (13/18 oz outdoor scrim vinyl), and flush straight-cut edge finish (no hems, no grommets).
     - Subsection **Required Materials & Fastening Hardware** detailing moving blankets, custom vinyl banner, 1-in. heavy-duty double-sided tape (Index [9]), 1-in. greenhouse snap clamps (Index [8]), surface degreaser, and personnel requirements (2 minimum, 3 recommended).
     - Subsection **Step-by-Step Vinyl Installation Procedure** spanning Steps 1 through 7:
       - Step 1: Staging and Surface Protection (moving blankets on swept shop floor, vinyl face down).
       - Step 2: Frame Placement and Bleed Alignment (face-down EMT frame centered over vinyl with equal bleed reveal on all four sides).
       - Step 3: Perimeter Double-Sided Tape Application (alcohol wipe-down is optional—weaker bond to tubing preferred for easier seasonal removal; 16x 4-in. strips of 1-in. tape on rear tubing: 5 per long rail, 3 per short rail).
       - Step 4: Long-Side Tensioning and Initial Wrap (first long side wrapped to tape; opposite long side pulled taut across frame and adhered to opposing tape).
       - Step 5: Short-Side Dual-Operator Tensioning and Wrap (two operators simultaneously pulling outward on opposite end rails and adhering to end tape strips).
       - Step 6: Greenhouse Snap Clamp Installation (16x 1-in. clamps snapped over vinyl at every tape location: 5 per long rail, 3 per short rail).
       - Step 7: Final Tension and Mechanical Clearance Inspection (upright visual check for wrinkle-free tension and deployment hinge/clip clearance verification).
     - Subsection **Vinyl Removal & End-of-Season Teardown Protocol:**
       - Mandatory caution: NEVER attempt vinyl or tape removal unless ambient temperature is strictly above 80°F (27°C); peeling at lower temperatures causes material tearing and damages the vinyl.
       - End-of-season procedure: defer removal until summer band camp of the following season (late-fall removal risks vinyl tearing from cold-stiffened adhesive); stage frames in direct sunlight for a few minutes to soften tape adhesive; verify >80°F (warm shop or use heat gun on low if not in sun); remove 16 greenhouse snap clamps; peel vinyl wrap slowly at a shallow angle; strip remaining tape from bare conduit.
