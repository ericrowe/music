# Sideline Screen Visual & Figure Style Guide

## 1. Framing & Background
- **Aspect & Canvas:** Standard horizontal layout, light neutral or white background.
- **Framing & Padding:** Maintain consistent 12–16 pt outer margin on technical drawings.
- **Border:** Clean, thin 0.5–1 pt border where appropriate.

## 2. Color System & Connection Coding
- **Red Markers (`#D32F2F`):** Fixed rigid connections that receive self-drilling screws (J).
- **Blue Markers (`#1976D2`):** Rotational hinge interfaces (must NOT receive screws; free to rotate).
- **Green Markers / Blocks (`#388E3C`):** 3D-printed Hinged Arm Clips (I) and operational guides.
- **Gray Hatching:** Offcut / scrap areas on cut schedule diagrams.

## 3. Labeling Conventions
- **Component Letters:** Bold sans-serif capital letters (A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J) enclosed in clean circles or adjacent leader callouts.
- **Dimensions:** Display imperial units with consistent notation (e.g., `92.5 in.`, `3/4-in. EMT`).
- **Typography:** Arial / Helvetica sans-serif; 10 pt callouts, 12 pt stage titles.

## 4. Alt Text Standard
- Every figure must provide complete, descriptive alt text explaining geometry, tube IDs, connector types, fastening points, and orientation for accessibility.
