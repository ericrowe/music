### Core Program Philosophy
> *"The focus is always on the students' performance, not the props. Props exist strictly to support and enhance the visual spectacle of the students' musicianship and marching. If conditions become hazardous or timing breaks down, props are abandoned or held back without hesitation. Nothing we do on the prop crew will ever compromise a student's safety or their competitive show."*

## 🛑 Wind Safety & Absolute NO-GO Threshold

Operating at Colorado Springs altitude (6,500 ft ASL) presents severe aeromechanical wind hazards in stadium bowls.

- **ABSOLUTE NO-GO THRESHOLD (>20 MPH):** If sustained winds exceed **20 mph** OR peak gusts are forecasted/measured at **25–30 mph**, **PROPS DO NOT LEAVE THE TRUCK/TRAILER**. Props remain locked in the equipment trailer or behind stadium bleachers. The band performs a clean visual drill without props.
- **Tiered Ballasting Schedule:**
  - **Tier 0 (0–8 mph Calm):** Duck Blinds: 1 bag (15 lb) on rear rail C; Backdrops: 0 bags (153 lb dry prop).
  - **Tier 1 (8–12 mph Normal):** Duck Blinds: 2 bags (30 lb) on rear rail C; Backdrops: 4 bags (60 lb: 2 per wing post).
  - **Tier 2 (12–18 mph Advisory):** Duck Blinds: 3 bags (45 lb) on rear rail C; Backdrops: 6 bags (90 lb: 3 per wing post).
  - **Tier 3 (18–22 mph High-Wind):** Duck Blinds: 4 bags (60 lb) on rear rail C; Backdrops: 9 bags (135 lb: 6 on wing posts + 3 on rear rail for 3.46-ft leverage).
  - **Tier 4 (>20 mph sust. / >25 mph gusts):** **ABSOLUTE NO-GO.**
