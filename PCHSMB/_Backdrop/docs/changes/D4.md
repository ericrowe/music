## Changes

1. **Full Format Conversion to Typst (`Backdrop_Assembly_Guide.typ`):**
   - Converted entire manual from legacy DOCX to high-performance, reproducible Typst typesetting format.
   - Standardized on exact 24-page modular document budget conforming to Universal PCHSMB Document Architecture (`PCHSMB/AGENTS.md`).
   - Implemented prefix page numbering: `A-` (Operations, 7 pages), `B-` (Construction, 12 pages), `C-` (Placard, 1 page), `C.1-` (Placement Card, 1 page), and `D-` (Volunteer Guide, 1 page).
   - Suppressed running headers on all appendix cover pages and single-sheet placards.

2. **Standardized Semicircular Wind Relief Cut Specifications:**
   - Standardized geometry on true semicircular cuts: radius $R = 4.0"$, horizontal hinge chord $8.0"$, downward drop $4.0"$.
   - Adopted primary 8-cut grid (2 rows × 4 columns; $Y = 96"$ and $Y = 72"$; $X = 19.2"$, $38.4"$, $57.6"$, $76.8"$), yielding 1.40 sq ft vent area (1.75% of face), reducing $C_d$ from 1.20 to 1.02, and elevating safe gust threshold to 22.5 mph at Colorado Springs elevation (6,500 ft ASL).
   - Retained approved minimal 6-cut variant (2 rows × 3 columns; 1.05 sq ft vent area).
   - Mandated 3/8" (10 mm) rotary leather/gasket punch holes at hinge chord ends to arrest stress concentration ($K_t \ge 3.0$) and prevent banner tearing.
   - Enforced upper venting zone concentration (upper 40%–60% of prop) and minimum 12" frame clearance.

3. **Page Budget Optimization & Zero-Orphan Layout:**

   - **Figure Numbering:** Set `#set figure(numbering: none)` and added canonical bold labels (`Figure 1.` through `Figure 9.`) to eliminate auto-numbering clashes.
