1. **Appendix A (Field Operations Manual):**

   - **Section 1.4 (Ballasting System, Tiered Wind Schedule, and Double-Bagging Protocol):**
     - Expanded into complete engineering ballasting manual with four dedicated subsections.
     - Added formal **Table: Standardized Tiered Ballasting & Wind Speed Schedule**:
       - *Tier 0 (Calm Operations, 0–8 mph, gusts ≤10 mph):* 0 bags unballasted (0 lbs total ballast, 153 lbs prop weight, 10.0 mph max safe gust, FoS ≥ 2.07). Mandatory upgrade to Tier 1 if wind exceeds 8 mph.
       - *Tier 1 (Normal Operations, 8–12 mph):* 4× 15-lb bags on wing posts (60 lbs total ballast, 213 lbs prop weight, 16.9 mph max gust).
       - *Tier 2 (Moderate Breeze / Advisory, 12–18 mph):* 6× 15-lb bags on wing posts (90 lbs total ballast, 243 lbs prop weight, 18.4 mph max gust).
       - *Tier 3 (High-Wind Operations, 18–22 mph):* 6× 15-lb wing bags (90 lbs) + 3× 15-lb bags laid flat across rear 2x4 rail (45 lbs) = 135 lbs total ballast (288 lbs prop weight, 22.5 mph max gust).
       - *Tier 4 (Severe Gale / Abort, >20 mph sustained / >25 mph gusts):* Mandatory field NO-GO / emergency withdrawal.
     - Added subsection on **High-Wind Rear-Rail Ballast Leverage Optimization**:
       - Explains critical forward-tipping asymmetry (deadweight of upright steel frame sits on front rail with zero restoring leverage).
       - Details the 3.46 ft lever arm of the rear rail vs 1.71 ft for wing posts, effectively doubling forward overturning resistance and balancing stability in both wind directions up to 22.5 mph.
       - Reasserts the 140-lb turf compaction ceiling (~75 lbs per caster wheel).
     - Added subsection on **Emergency Reserve Protocol (70-lb Tube Sand)**:
       - Permits 1–2 70-lb Sakrete tube sand bags (double-bagged in contractor-grade plastic) along rear lumber framing strictly as an emergency reserve if 15-lb bags are depleted.

      - Updated crew pairing from "exactly two" to "No fewer than two student handlers".

      - Updated Section 1.2 curb/ramp navigation and Rule 8.09 Falcon Stadium venue notes.
      - Updated Appendix C on-prop placard form fields and safety rules with the minimum-2 / 3-4 handler requirement.

## Implementation notes

- Standardized on 15-lb double-bagged handle sandbags for normal and high-wind operations (up to 9 bags = 135 lbs).
- Placing 3 supplemental sandbags across the rear 2x4 rail provides a 3.46 ft lever arm to front casters (vs 1.71 ft for wing posts), equalizing forward and backward tipping stability up to 22.5 mph in Colorado Springs.
- The 70-lb Sakrete tube sand is retained strictly as an emergency staging reserve.
- 3/8-in. punch holes at flap hinge corners eliminate sharp stress-raising corners, preventing tear propagation across the scrim vinyl.
