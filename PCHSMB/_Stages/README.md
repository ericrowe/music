# PCHSMB Performance Stage & Staircase System

The **PCHSMB Performance Stage System** consists of **four (4) modular rolling performance stages** and **four (4) mobile staircases** engineered for the Pine Creek High School Marching Band. The system serves as elevated performance podiums for soloists, visual ensembles, and color guard features during competitive field shows (including the *2026 Continuum* production).

Each stage platform is composed of two independent rolling half-units (Front Half and Back Half) that roll onto the field during the timed entrance, dock together at precision field marks, and roll off through the end zone upon show completion.

## 🔍 The 6.0-Inch Inter-Section Gap Challenge

When the modular stage sections roll together on the field, physical **approximate 6.0-inch (nominal 152 mm) open gaps** exist between the walking decks. There are **six (6) gaps across the fleet**, each measuring **82.0 inches long** ($6\text{ ft } 10\text{ in.}$, $41.0\text{ linear ft}$ total).

> [!WARNING]
> **PERFORMER FALL & ANKLE INJURY HAZARD:**  
> An open 6.0-inch gap across a performance stage is wide enough for athletic/marching footwear to penetrate completely. Because the stage uses a printed vinyl graphic top cover with a velcro seam flap, an unbridged gap creates a hazardous **"false floor"** that will punch through if stepped on.

### Engineering Analysis & Solution
We have completed an exhaustive structural and ergonomic study evaluating bridging methods, live performer point loads (375 lbf dynamic strike), trip hazards, and chassis racking:

👉 **[Read the Full Gap Bridge Engineering Analysis & Recommendations](docs/references/STAGE_GAP_BRIDGE_ANALYSIS.md)**

#### Summary of Recommendations:
1. **Option 1: Flush Drop-In T-Bridge with Sub-Deck Ledgers (Primary Standard):**
   - Combines a nominal 2x6 Doug Fir core with a 3/4″ Baltic birch top cap resting on recessed frame ledgers.
   - Achieves a **100% flush, seamless walking surface** with **zero trip hazard** under the vinyl flap.
2. **Mandatory Inter-Chassis Clamping:**
   - Two over-center heavy-duty draw latches per stage platform lock the Front and Back chassis together under tension, guaranteeing a fixed 6.0″ gap under all dynamic live loads.
3. **Option 2: Hinged Flip-Deck Bridge (Fastest Setup):**
   - Continuous piano-hinged flap that flips over in 1 second during field entry.
